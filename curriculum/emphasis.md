# Emphasis plan

How the system shifts weight toward what he is good at and interested in,
without quietly writing off everything else. Reviewed monthly, after the
aspiration check-in.

## The policy: floor and surplus

Two ways to get this wrong, and both are common.

| Mistake | What happens |
|---|---|
| Double down on strengths | A 12-year-old's "talent" signal is mostly noise — a good teacher, a lucky term. Specialising on it closes doors that were never really open or shut |
| Ignore the signal entirely | The system stops feeling like it is listening, and the interest it could have used goes elsewhere |

So: **every subject keeps a floor, and only the surplus moves.**

| Layer | Rule |
|---|---|
| Floor | The school programme, for every subject, every week. Never reduced, whatever the numbers say |
| Surplus | Optional sessions, deeper problems, boss levels, enrichment from Kolibri — these follow interest and progress |

The emphasis is therefore visible in what he does *extra*, never in what he
stops doing. That is also the sentence to say to him when he asks why he still
has to do chemistry: «Хімію ти не кидаєш. Але додаткові години йдуть туди, де
в тебе йде краще».

## Signals, and what each is worth

Read from the logs monthly. None of these is reliable alone.

| Signal | Source | Reads as |
|---|---|---|
| Solved without hints, trend over weeks | `tasks[].hints` | Progress |
| Gaps closed | `gaps_closed` | Real learning, not just performance |
| Anki failure rate by deck | `anki-mcp` review stats | Retention — the honest one |
| `interesting` answers | `feedback` | Interest, self-reported |
| Difficulty he chooses | `state/today.md` history | Confidence in that subject |
| Which subject he starts first | Log order within a day | Interest, revealed rather than stated |
| `why_asked` frequency | Session logs | Motivation problem in that subject |

The last two are worth more than the self-reported ones. What he does first and
what he asks «навіщо» about say more than what he answers to «цікаво?».
