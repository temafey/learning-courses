# Theme pack — Star Wars

Slug: `star-wars`. Template for every other pack.

## Vocabulary

| Category | Usable in problems |
|---|---|
| Craft | X-wing, TIE, «Тисячолітній сокіл», зоряний руйнівник, спідер |
| People | Люк, Лея, Хан, Асока, дроїд R2, дроїд C-3PO |
| Places | Татуїн, Хот, Ендор, Корусант, пояс астероїдів |
| Quantities | пальне, вантаж, відстань у парсеках, час до стрибка, заряд бластера, потужність щита |
| Groups | ескадрилья (12 машин), загін, флот |

Ukrainian spelling above is what the student sees. Keep it consistent across
subjects — the same craft should not be «Сокіл» in algebra and «Falcon» in
physics.

## Ready framings

| Subject | Shape |
|---|---|
| Алгебра | Fuel split between two craft; cargo distributed across a squadron; a ratio of speeds between two ships |
| Фізика | Acceleration of a speeder; braking distance; energy of a shield; relative motion of two craft |
| Хімія | Composition of fuel mixtures; proportions in an alloy for hull plating |
| Англійська | A short mission briefing to translate or rewrite in a given tense |

Every one of these is a normal school problem with the names changed. That is
the whole intent.

## «Міф чи фізика?» seeds

Each is a scene the student has watched, checkable against a grade-7 textbook
law. Ask what is wrong, then let him reason before answering.

| Scene | Law it tests |
|---|---|
| A fighter brakes sharply in space and stops dead | Newton's first law — nothing to brake against |
| Explosions in space are heard as bangs | Sound needs a medium |
| A ship banks into a turn like an aeroplane | Aerodynamic lift does not exist in vacuum |
| The Falcon "makes the Kessel Run in 12 parsecs" | Parsec is a unit of distance, not of time |
| An asteroid field packed so densely that ships weave between rocks | Real asteroid belts are mostly empty space |

The last two are the best of the set: the student almost certainly believes
both, and correcting them costs the film nothing.

## Do not

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| The Force, hyperspace mechanics, anything with invented physics | There is no textbook law to check it against |
| Problems needing plot knowledge to parse | Breaks rule 2 of `docs/engagement-system.md` §1 |
| Fictional units — parsec is fine as a distance, «кредити» are not a currency he can reason about | Adds a conversion step that teaches nothing |
| Stating in-universe technology as real science | The pack decorates; the textbook rules |
