# Proposed rewards

Ideas from the student, appended by whichever session he mentions them in. The
parent decides: approved items move into `rewards/catalogue.md` with a price
and a band, declined ones get a reason and stay here.

Being able to propose is part of the point. A catalogue written entirely by a
parent rewards what the parent thinks he wants.

| Date | Item | Est. cost | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## How a proposal is handled

1. He names something during any session. The model appends a row here and
   tells him his parent decides. It does not estimate a price and does not
   guess whether it will be approved.
2. The parent fills in cost, decision and reason.
3. Approved — the item is copied into the catalogue with a price in points.
4. Declined — the reason is written down. "Too expensive" is a fine reason;
   silence is not, and an unexplained refusal is what makes the next proposal
   not happen.
