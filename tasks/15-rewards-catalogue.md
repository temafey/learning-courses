# 15 — Fill the reward catalogue and agree the rules

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | `todo` |

## Goal

A catalogue the parent can afford and the student actually wants, plus one
conversation that sets the rules before the first point is earned.

## Steps — the catalogue

1. Decide the four budget ceilings in `rewards/catalogue.md` once, calmly.
   Deciding them item by item under negotiation is how a reward system becomes
   expensive.
2. Ask the student what he would want at each level. Write down what he says,
   not what seems reasonable — an item chosen for him motivates nobody.
3. Fill the items, leaving **prices blank**. Prices are set in task 16, once
   the real daily point range is known.
4. Mark each item approved or not. An unapproved item is invisible to the
   tutor.

## Steps — the conversation

Have it once, in person, before the system runs. Cover four things and no more:

| Point | Wording that works |
|---|---|
| Points are for showing up and trying, not for being right | «Бали за те, що ти сів і працював, а не за правильну відповідь» |
| Asking for a hint costs nothing | «Підказка не забирає балів. Взагалі» |
| AI help is allowed for understanding, not for submitting | «Просити пояснити — можна. Вставляти чужу відповідь — ні» |
| A copied answer is not punished, it is re-taught | «Не зарахується, і тему пояснимо ще раз. Балів не знімаємо» |

The fourth point is the one to say out loud. The system does not detect
cheating and does not try to — it asks «а чому саме так?» and re-teaches what
cannot be explained. Saying that plainly removes the incentive to test it and
removes the fear of being falsely accused.

## Done when

- [ ] Four budget ceilings written down
- [ ] At least two items per tier, chosen by the student
- [ ] Every item marked approved or not
- [ ] Prices left blank, deliberately
- [ ] The four rules said out loud and understood
