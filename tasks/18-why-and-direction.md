## Goal

Every topic the student meets this term has an honest written answer to
«навіщо мені це», the first monthly check-in has been run, and the first
emphasis plan exists in `curriculum/emphasis.md`. Until this is done the tutor
improvises its answers, which is exactly what `curriculum/why/README.md`
forbids.

## Steps

1. Open the textbook for each subject and list the topics of the current term
   in `curriculum/why/<subject>.md`. `algebra.md` exists as the pattern; the
   remaining subjects need the same table.
2. Fill the `Навіщо` column in the order of preference from
   `curriculum/why/README.md`: what breaks without it, where it appears in a
   direction he has named, what it unlocks, and — when none of those is
   honestly available — that it is a prerequisite and nothing more.

   Do not skip a topic because the honest answer is weak. A missing row is
   what makes the tutor invent one.
3. Check the rows in `curriculum/why/algebra.md` against the actual textbook.
   They were written from general knowledge of the grade-7 programme and are
   marked unverified in `docs/engagement-system.md` §13.
4. Run the first monthly check-in from `prompts/aspirations.md` in the `День`
   project. Five minutes, on a day with no lesson attached.
5. Confirm `state/aspirations.md` was filled with his own wording and that the
   History table gained a row.
6. If the direction he named is missing from `curriculum/directions.md`, add
   it — four rows, the same shape as the existing ones, written honestly. Do
   this before the next check-in.
7. After two weeks of logs exist, run the parent-facing numbers table from
   `prompts/aspirations.md`, then write the first emphasis plan into
   `curriculum/emphasis.md` yourself. The floor stays the school programme;
   only the surplus moves.

## Done when

- [ ] Every subject has a `curriculum/why/<subject>.md` covering this term
- [ ] No topic row is blank, including the ones with only a prerequisite answer
- [ ] `curriculum/why/algebra.md` has been checked against the textbook
- [ ] The first check-in ran and `state/aspirations.md` has a History row
- [ ] Every direction he named has a row in `curriculum/directions.md`
- [ ] `curriculum/emphasis.md` names where the surplus goes and why
- [ ] A session log has appeared containing a non-empty `why_asked`

The second row is the one to watch over the term. A subject where most topics
answer "prerequisite only" is not a failure of the table — it is a real finding
about that subject's place in the programme, and it belongs in the weekly
report rather than being written away.
